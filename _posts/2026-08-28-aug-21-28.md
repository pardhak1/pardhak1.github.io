### This week's stories

**Dutch Bros and 7 Brew in a bidding war for 50+ Salad and Go sites**
Court-supervised auction set for Thursday. The real prize isn't the real estate — it's denying those locations to a direct beverage rival. Worth watching who blinks first on price.

**Jack in the Box poaches Taco Bell's Chief Brand Officer as incoming CEO**
Taylor Montgomery joins as president with a path to CEO within 12 months. Notable that JIB reached into Yum's bench for turnaround leadership instead of promoting internally — a signal of how much rebuilding the brand thinks it needs.

**Starbucks cuts 200+ corporate roles, consolidating support functions in Nashville**
More than half the cuts came from employees declining to relocate — Starbucks appears to be using the move as a forcing function to reset its support org, not just cut costs. Nashville also puts the company closer to its higher-growth South/East expansion markets.

### Also this week
- Blank Street Coffee opened its first LA-area unit in Studio City — 106th location overall, with a second LA-area site planned in Beverly Hills
- Wendy's named Tariq Hassan (ex-McDonald's U.S. Chief Marketing and Customer Experience Officer) as its new Chief Marketing and Customer Growth Officer
- Papa Johns brought on KM Capital as its Mexico franchise partner (44 restaurants) and refinanced 28 Orlando locations to two new ownership groups

### Commodities watch
- **Beef:** Live cattle futures down ~7% over the past month, an 11-month low as of Aug 28 — a margin tailwind for burger-heavy names like MCD and QSR's Burger King heading into fall.
- **Coffee:** Arabica hit a 7.5-month high this week as Brazil's harvest runs behind pace, tightening supply — worth watching for any coffee-exposed QSR menu lines.
